How to read this. Published findings come from human post-mortem PD studies, extracted from Europe PMC abstracts. Each one is kept only if its quoted sentence appears verbatim in the abstract. Prefrontal transcriptomes across parkinsonian disorders come from ParkDB (Brain 2026). Mechanism pathways come from the PD map (LCSB, University of Luxembourg, CC BY 4.0). Imaging effect sizes are from ENIGMA-PD (Laansma et al. 2021), and Braak stage-related regions follow Keo et al. 2020. Region mapping is approximate wherever a paper reports a lobe or a broad area. Co-location of findings is an association, not evidence of mechanism. This site is for research use only, not for clinical use.
